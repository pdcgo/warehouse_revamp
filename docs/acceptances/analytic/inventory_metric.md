# Acceptance — analytic / inventory_metric

The five inventory metrics on **Analitik**, all in units — *Restok* (ordered on the day it is raised or edited and taken
back if cancelled, lost on the way, good units accepted, broken at the door, missing, and the courier's COD),
*Transfer* (sent, received, in transit), *Stok retur* (back on the shelf, broken, never came back), *Stok siap* (ready
at the period's edge) and *Stok hilang* (written off). A rupiah value beside a count is the seller's money and is
not shown to a warehouse, except ready and lost stock and the COD it paid. Rules: [guide.md](../guide.md)
